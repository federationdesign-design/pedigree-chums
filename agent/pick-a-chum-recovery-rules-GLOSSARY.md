# Recovery Rules: glossary (section 8) built from the live copy

Session item 5. Every unusual or character-specific term currently in the Pick
a Chum copy, with a plain definition, a child-friendly definition, and an
example (drawn from the actual copy wherever possible). Multi-word entries are
flagged in the "Phrase?" column, per your instruction.

Source of terms: the live user-facing copy fields, read this session:
`collie-responses.json` (`template`), `copy-components.json` (`line`),
`transfers.json` (`exampleLine`), `general-knowledge.json` (`collieObservation`),
`faq.json` (`canonicalAnswer`). No glossary structure exists in the code yet, so
this is net-new; once you approve it, it moves into the workbook as the
section-8 store (plain def, child-friendly def, example, optional in-character
wording).

Scope note: I have included coined product terms, the Collie's Geordie dialect,
his working-dog/herding metaphor vocabulary, the grammar and keyboard words he
uses about himself (the ones section 8 exists to protect, e.g. "verb"), the
commercial terms, a few product-description words, the four dog characters, and
the one external service named in safety copy. I have left out ordinary words
that happen to be dry or formal ("jurisdiction", "improvisation") since those
are tone, not vocabulary a visitor cannot parse. Anything borderline is
included and marked, so you can cut rather than hunt.

## Coverage gaps and judgement calls left for Steve

- **Terms with no plain in-world definition yet.** "Hot Dog Mode" is defined
  from context (a mode of the game) but the copy never states what it actually
  changes; if a child asks, the current answer is a token,
  `{{hot_dog_mode_answer}}`. You own that answer. Flagging so it is not assumed.
- **Coined jokes as glossary entries.** "Herdability" is a one-off joke, not a
  real term. I included it because a literal-minded or younger visitor might
  ask. Cut it if you would rather the dog just not explain a joke.
- **Price and discount figures** are deliberately shown as PLACEHOLDER-PRICE
  here (house rule: no restating campaign numbers in a review doc). The
  glossary in the workbook should point at the campaign source, not hardcode a
  number.
- **Childline row** must be kept in lockstep with the approved safety copy. I
  have not altered any safety wording; the definition is descriptive only.
